Element: pad footings (example).

What they look like:

- On a foundation plan, a pad footing is a rectangle, usually dashed or hatched, around a column, with a mark such as F1 or PF2 next to it.
- The plan size (length and width) is often written next to the mark, for example "F1 1500 x 1200".
- The depth is usually not on the plan. Look for a footing schedule or a section through the footing.

What to submit, one observation per footing mark per storey:

- `mark`: the mark as printed.
- `length_m` and `width_m`: the longer and shorter plan dimensions in metres.
- `depth_m`: the depth in metres, from the schedule or section.
- `count`: how many footings with this mark you can see in the area.

If the same mark appears several times, submit it once with the right count.
